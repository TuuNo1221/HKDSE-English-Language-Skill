## Useful page ranges

- **Sentence patterns with Content page, pp. 1–2** gives all 65 numbered patterns. **Pages 3–9** regroup them by function and include advice patterns. Match question and answer sheets by code and exercise number, since their PDF page counts differ.
- **Writing formats, pp. 1–2** introduces task analysis. **Pages 4–15** cover genres, openings, body layouts and endings. **Pages 16–18** cover debate, complaint and adjustment.
- **Theory of decoding passages & MIINAH, pp. 1–2** covers structure and the source diagrams. **Pages 3–8** expand each of the six evidence categories. See [genres](genres.md) for how to use them with reliable examples.
- **Paper 3 Format PEARLS, p. 1** summarises PEARLS and two layouts. **Pages 2–5** give proposal/email, article/report, letters, and speech in that order. The latter pages include handwritten fields.
- **HKDSE Paper 4 Speaking Examination, pp. 2–4** covers format and planning. **Pages 5–6** show example discussion themes and individual questions. **Pages 7–11** cover interaction phrases and individual response. **Pages 12–13** cover delivery and group behaviour.
- **tools of language, pp. 2–18** covers characterisation, description, headlines, plot and narrative openings and endings. **Pages 19–25** cover sentence openers and storytelling expressions. **Pages 26–41** include figurative devices, emotion, tone, mood, character traits, appearance, adjective order, and colour idioms. **Pages 42–51** include punctuation, sample writing, and clause and connective practice.

The handouts include historical tasks and classroom conventions. For the actual test, use the instructions for the paper and year. Keep ambiguous scan text marked as uncertain and prefer the page image over OCR when exact wording matters.
